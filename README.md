# Exceptional Women in STEM & Medicine

**Discoverers, Pioneer Theorists, Experimentalists & Clinical Game Changers**

An interactive, self-contained web directory and curated multi-format dataset celebrating **pioneering women across STEM and medical disciplines**. 

Developed by the [Armache Lab](https://www.armachelab.psu.edu) at The Pennsylvania State University (Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology).

---

## Overview

Each entry highlights a scientist's primary subspecialty, landmark scientific discovery or clinical breakthrough, birthplace, country/continent of birth and work, disciplinary tags, and a direct biography/reference link.
